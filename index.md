# User Data Deletion

- **Game:** Color trail behind a black ball Relax (the "Game")
- **Developer:** Michail Eshmakov, independent developer
- **Publisher:** Mediacube

## What data the Game stores

The Game does not use Facebook Login and does not store any data on our own servers. The only data it saves is your game progress: the levels you have completed and the level you reached. This progress is kept in the player data storage that Facebook provides to Instant Games. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](../rainball-privacy-policy/).

## How to delete your data

Remove the Game from your Facebook account. This deletes the progress that Facebook stores for the Game.

**In the Facebook app:**

1. Tap the menu, then **Settings & privacy** → **Settings**.
2. Open **Apps and websites**.
3. Open the **Instant Games** tab.
4. Find **Color trail behind a black ball Relax** and tap **Remove**.
5. Confirm the removal.

**On facebook.com:**

1. Click your profile picture, then **Settings & privacy** → **Settings**.
2. Open **Apps and websites**.
3. Open the **Instant Games** tab.
4. Find **Color trail behind a black ball Relax**, click **Remove** and confirm.

Facebook may change the names of these menu items. If you cannot find them, search for "Apps and websites" in the Facebook settings.
